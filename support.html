Support

Need help with REIGN? Found a bug or have an idea for the game?

REIGN is currently in beta testing, and feedback is welcome.

Contact the Developer

For technical issues, questions, or feedback, please contact Magne Sortland Studio:

magne.sortland.studio@gmail.com

When reporting a bug, please include your device model, Android or iOS version, and a brief description of the problem.

Account and Data Deletion

To request deletion of your REIGN account and associated personal information, contact Magne Sortland Studio:

Request account deletion by email

Include your player name and guild name, if known. We may request additional information to verify ownership of the account. Never send passwords or sign-in tokens. You do not need to reinstall the app to submit a request.

Deletion covers your guest account, player profile, settlement progress and associated personal information. Shared gameplay records may remain after information identifying your account has been removed.

Requests are handled within applicable legal time limits. If information must be retained for security or legal reasons, we will explain what is retained, why, and for how long when responding to your request.

Beta Testing

REIGN is available to invited testers through Apple's TestFlight and Google Play testing. You can report issues using the email address above.

If you use TestFlight, you can also submit feedback directly through Apple's TestFlight app.
