Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

REIGN is a mobile strategy game developed by Magne Sortland Studio. This policy explains how information is handled in the current beta version.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The current beta version of REIGN stores gameplay information locally on your device. This may include your chosen player name, civilization, game progress, settings, and other gameplay-related information.

This information is used to provide the game's functionality and is not currently transmitted to a REIGN-operated server.

2. Accounts and Online Services

The current beta version does not require an online REIGN account and does not currently provide server-based multiplayer functionality.

Future versions may introduce online accounts, multiplayer features, and cloud storage. This privacy policy will be updated before those features are introduced.

3. Analytics and Advertising

REIGN does not currently integrate third-party advertising or analytics services for tracking player activity.

4. Beta Testing Through TestFlight

If you access REIGN through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may process beta testing information, including installation details, crash reports, and feedback, according to Apple's own privacy practices.

For more information, please review Apple's Privacy Policy .

5. Data Retention

Gameplay data stored locally may remain on your device until it is removed through the app or the app is uninstalled, depending on the device's operating system and backup settings.

6. Children's Privacy

REIGN is not specifically designed for children. The current beta does not intentionally collect personal information through an online REIGN service.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as REIGN develops. Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact Magne Sortland Studio through our support page.
