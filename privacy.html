Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

REIGN is a mobile strategy game developed by Magne Sortland Studio. Magne Sortland Studio is responsible for the personal information processed to operate REIGN. This policy explains how information is handled in the current beta, including online gameplay, guilds and optional phone notifications.

1. Guest Accounts and Gameplay Information

REIGN automatically creates a guest account using Supabase authentication. You do not need to provide an email address or password. Your account receives a unique identifier, and a sign-in session is stored on your device so the game can recognize you when you return. A guest account is not completely anonymous: its identifier links your gameplay information to the same account over time.

We store your chosen player name, civilization, settlement resources, building levels, units, development, power points, pending orders and relevant gameplay history on our Supabase backend. This includes information needed for scouting, movement, combat, battle reports, guild membership and guild invitations.

This information is used to save progress, process the shared game ticks, calculate outcomes, support multiplayer features and prevent players from manipulating game state. Please avoid using sensitive information or a name you do not want other players to see as your player or guild name.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

REIGN also stores information locally, including your sign-in session, cached gameplay data, settings, notification preferences, scouting report archive and information used to prepare the Jarl's Advisor summary.

Detailed scouting reports are saved on your device after delivery. The server temporarily holds reports for delivery and retains the discovery and gameplay information needed for scouting and attacks. Deleting a local report does not undo a discovery or remove records needed to operate the game.

3. Information Visible to Other Players

Other players may see your player name, civilization, level, power points, guild and other information made available through gameplay. Guild members can see the guild roster, relevant member statistics and military activity shared within the guild. Guild names, banners, member counts and combined power points appear in guild rankings.

Scouting, incoming attack warnings and battle reports may reveal additional information, such as army composition, resource estimates or battle results, according to the game's rules. Your full private settlement data and sign-in credentials are not made generally available to other players.

4. Phone Notifications

If you enable notifications, REIGN may notify you about completed orders, detected attacks, allied reinforcements, food shortages and requested resource availability alerts. Depending on the alert and platform, notifications may be scheduled on your device or delivered remotely.

Remote delivery uses Expo's push service and Apple Push Notification service on iOS or Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android. We store a device push token linked to your account and your notification preferences. Tokens and the information needed to deliver an alert may be sent to these providers. Alert content may include player names, event details, remaining ticks and an order identifier.

You can manage notifications in REIGN's settings and your device settings. Notification previews may be visible on your lock screen, depending on your device settings.

5. Service Providers and Technical Information

Supabase provides REIGN's authentication and hosted database services. Expo, Apple and Google support app distribution or notification delivery, as applicable. Infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, request details, error information and operational logs to deliver and secure their services.

Relevant provider information is available in the Supabase Privacy Policy , Expo Privacy Policy , Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy . Provider processing may take place outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Contact us for information about the locations and transfer safeguards relevant to your data.

6. Analytics, Advertising and Beta Testing

REIGN does not currently integrate third-party advertising or player-behaviour analytics SDKs. Operational and security logs used to run the online service are separate from advertising tracking.

If you use Apple's TestFlight or Google Play testing, Apple or Google may process installation information, diagnostics, crash reports and feedback under their own privacy practices. If you contact us for support, we use the information you provide to respond and investigate the issue.

7. Why We Process Information

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, we process information necessary to provide the game and its online features to perform our agreement with you. We rely on our legitimate interests to protect the service, prevent abuse and resolve technical problems. Optional phone notifications are enabled at your request, with your device's permission. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent.

8. Retention, Starting Over and Deletion

We retain account and gameplay information while it is needed to provide the game, maintain shared game history, handle support or protect the service. Retention depends on the type of information and its purpose. Beta progress may be reset during testing.

The "Start over" option in Settings removes your current game profile and associated settlement and clears local gameplay data and preferences. It does not necessarily delete the underlying authentication account, provider logs, backups or information already included in other players' reports. To request deletion of remaining personal information, contact us through our support page.

Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete server-side information. Local data may be removed or retained according to your operating system and backup settings. Losing your guest sign-in session may prevent you from accessing the same account again.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction, deletion or a copy of your personal information, and may have rights to restrict or object to certain processing. Contact us through our support page. We may need information to verify that a request relates to your guest account; we will not ask for your sign-in tokens or passwords.

You may also complain to your local data protection authority. In Norway, this is Datatilsynet .

10. Children's Privacy

REIGN is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information without any legally required parental permission, contact us so we can investigate and handle the information appropriately.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may change as REIGN develops. Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before making material changes to how personal information is used.

12. Contact

For questions or privacy requests, contact Magne Sortland Studio through our support page.
